# Attribution and operational boundary

The knowledge files in this package are original, shortened Chinese summaries
of public U.S. Food and Drug Administration and U.S. Department of Energy
materials. Source URLs and revisions are recorded in `knowledge/metadata.yaml`
and `LICENSES.yaml`. No agency logo or protected mark is distributed.

The FDA Food Code is a model offered for adoption by responsible jurisdictions;
it is not automatically the applicable law at every installation. This package
uses the operator's documented site threshold and does not determine regulatory
compliance.

This package diagnoses evidence for operational cause categories. It does not
decide whether food is safe, saleable, reusable, or subject to disposal; it does
not provide HACCP certification, regulatory advice, automatic repair, or
refrigerant-handling instructions. It is not endorsed by FDA, DOE, EPA, or any
other government body.
